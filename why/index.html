Questions

Questions families ask

Is Road to Kakul official?

No. It is an independent preparation app. You apply only on the official websites: joinpakarmy.gov.pk, joinpaknavy.gov.pk and joinpaf.gov.pk.

Can an app replace an academy?

ISSB itself advises candidates not to join coaching centres — see the proof above. What helps is daily practice of the tests, a fit body and confidence in speaking. The app gives you the practice, in your own words and with no set answers to learn by heart; running, group tasks with friends and speaking in front of people you can do yourself, for free.

Does it guarantee selection?

No one can. Selection is on merit at every stage. Anyone who promises selection for money is a fraud.

Which courses is it for?

PMA Long Course, Technical Cadet Course, Medical Cadet (Army Medical Colleges), Lady Cadet Course, PN Cadet (Permanent Commission) and GD Pilot (Permanent Commission). Each has its own syllabus, standards and guides in the app.

Is it in Urdu?

Yes. Every screen and guide works in English and Urdu, and so does the interview coach.

How do I pay, and is it safe?

Inside the app with Easypaisa: scan the QR code, pay, and enter the transaction ID from your receipt. We never ask for a card, a password or any document.

What if I cannot pay?

Share the app to earn free days, or ask for an honour scholarship inside the app.
