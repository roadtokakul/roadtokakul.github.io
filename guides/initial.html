Initial tests explained

After registration you are called to your AS&RC for the initial tests. Each stage filters candidates, and your marks count towards final merit.

The order (typical)

  • Intelligence tests: verbal and non-verbal (computerised or written)
  • Academic test
  • Physical test
  • Initial medical
  • Initial interview
  • If you clear these, you receive a call letter for ISSB

How to prepare

  • Practise every day with a timer. Speed and accuracy both matter.
  • Verbal: series, analogies, odd one out, coding, directions, relations.
  • Non-verbal: figure series, rotation, odd figure, counting patterns.
  • Academic: English grammar and vocabulary, basic maths, general knowledge, Pakistan studies and Islamiat.
  • Follow the instructions given at the centre on time limits and marking.
