The OPI test (Officer Personality Inventory)

Dozens of statements about yourself: agree, disagree, or not sure.

The OPI is a questionnaire of statements about your habits and feelings. You mark each one Agree, Disagree or Not sure, against time. Many statements are asked twice in different words to check that you answer consistently.

How to answer

  • Answer how you really are, quickly. Your first honest answer is your best answer.
  • Be consistent. If you agree that you finish what you start, you cannot also agree that you leave tasks half done.
  • Use “Not sure” rarely. Too many look like you are hiding.
  • Do not try to look perfect. Nobody agrees with every good statement and disagrees with every bad one.

Try the OPI practice in the Psych lab — it shows you where your answers contradict each other.
