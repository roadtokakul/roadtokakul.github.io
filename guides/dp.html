The Deputy President’s interview

The long, friendly interview — and why honesty matters more than polish.

The interviewing officer (often called the Deputy President) talks to you for 20 to 40 minutes, usually on the first or second day. He has your biodata form in front of him and asks about your life in detail: family, studies, friends, sports, achievements, failures and your reasons for joining.

Why he is so friendly

A relaxed candidate shows his real self. The friendliness is real, but it is also a method: candidates who relax completely stop polishing their answers. That is the point — be honest from the first question, then it does not matter.

How to prepare

  • Know your biodata form by heart, every date and every mark. He will cross-check.
  • Think about your life honestly: what you did well, what you regret, what you learned.
  • Prepare for rapid questions: he may ask ten small questions in a row. Answer in order, calmly.
  • Keep up with the news for two weeks before ISSB.

Questions to think about

  • What do you regret most, and what did you learn from it?
  • Who is your best friend and why? What is his weakness?
  • Describe your daily routine.
  • What would you do if you are not recommended?
