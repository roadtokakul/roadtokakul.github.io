ISSB, day by day

ISSB (Inter Services Selection Board) usually runs over 4–5 days. The exact order varies by centre and batch, but this is the typical shape.

Arrival day

  • Reception, document checks, chest numbers, photographs
  • Filling in forms (biodata). Your interview is built on these — write the truth.

Psychologist's day

  • Opening address
  • Intelligence tests, then the screening-out result
  • Psychological tests: word association, sentence completion, picture story, self-description and others

GTO days (usually two)

  • Group discussion, group planning exercise, lecturette
  • Progressive group task, half group task
  • Individual obstacles, command task, final group task, group race
  • Mutual assessment
  • Interviews with the Deputy President and the psychologist run alongside

Conference day

  • All assessors meet; some candidates are re-interviewed
  • Results are announced and candidates depart
