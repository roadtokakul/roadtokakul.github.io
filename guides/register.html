Applying online, step by step

How online registration works for the Army, Navy and Air Force — and how to avoid fake adverts.

All three services take applications online on their official websites. The steps are similar.

1. Wait for the advert (newspapers, the official website, and News & dates in this app).

2. Open the official website and create an account with your own email and phone number.

3. Fill the form carefully — name exactly as on your CNIC/B-form, correct marks, correct date of birth.

4. Upload a clear photograph and documents if asked.

5. Choose your test centre / selection centre.

6. Submit and print or save your registration slip / admit card.

7. Appear for the initial test on the date given, with the documents listed.

Official websites

  • Army: joinpakarmy.gov.pk
  • Navy: joinpaknavy.gov.pk
  • Air Force: joinpaf.gov.pk

Never pay an agent to “guarantee” selection. Never share your account password. Real adverts are on .gov.pk websites.
