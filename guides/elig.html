Eligibility & registration

PMA Long Course is the regular route to a commission in the Pakistan Army. Registration usually opens twice a year. Always check the current advertisement — the figures below are from recent notices.

Who can apply

  • Male citizens of Pakistan, AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan domicile holders
  • Unmarried
  • Intermediate (FA/FSc/ICS/A-level or equivalent) with at least 60% marks; 55% for candidates from specified areas (such as AJK, Gilgit-Baltistan and the former FATA)
  • Age 17–22 years for Intermediate candidates, with up to 3 months relaxation
  • Graduates: 17–23 (2-year degree) or 17–24 (4-year degree)
  • Minimum height 5 ft 4 in (162.5 cm); weight in line with BMI

Who cannot apply

  • Anyone convicted by a court
  • Anyone who tampered with academic certificates
  • Anyone declared medically unfit by a military hospital or appeal medical board
  • Other cases listed in the advertisement (for example, earlier withdrawal from a training institution)

How to register

  • Online at joinpakarmy.gov.pk, or at your nearest Army Selection and Recruitment Centre (AS&RC)
  • Choose the AS&RC closest to you; your tests happen there
  • Print your admit card/roll number slip and keep copies of everything

Selection is merit-based. Anyone asking for money to ‘guarantee’ selection is a fraud — report them.
