What ISSB preparation really costs

Official facts, published prices and a cheaper way — every number with its source.

Many families believe selection needs an expensive academy. The board itself says otherwise. Here is what preparation really costs: the official facts first, then the published prices, each with where it came from and the day we checked it.

What ISSB itself says

“Over the years, many private institutions and coaching centers have been established in the country to train candidates for passing ISSB tests. You are advised not to join these for training and guidance. The training imparted in these centers is too restricted and brings in rigidity in thought process.” — ISSB guidelines for candidates, issb.gov.pk

What the board pays for

  • Your stay: “Selection board will provide you lodging and messing on government expenses.” A bed and your meals for the whole board, free.
  • Your travel: a travelling allowance from your home address — train fare, or bus fare when the road is shorter. (Candidates already serving in the forces travel on a railway warrant instead.)
  • The time: your interview and tests complete in four days, not counting the day you arrive.

Source: ISSB joining instructions, issb.gov.pk, clauses 1, 5 and 24.

The cheaper way that works

1. Read ISSB’s own guidelines for candidates on issb.gov.pk. They are free and come straight from the board.

2. Practise every test, every day: word association, sentence completion, picture stories, situation reactions, the OPI and the interview. Practice makes your answers quicker and more honest. It cannot buy a result.

3. Train your body at home with a plan and a stopwatch: the run, push-ups, sit-ups and chin-ups.

4. Practise group tasks and talks with friends: a ground, a few planks, a rope, and everyone takes a turn to lead.
