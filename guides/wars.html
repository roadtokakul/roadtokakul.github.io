Pakistan's wars & major operations

Interviewers often ask about the wars and recent operations. Know the year, the main fronts, one hero and one lesson for each.

1947–48: First Kashmir war

  • Tribal lashkars and then regular troops fought in Kashmir. A UN ceasefire came on 1 January 1949, creating the ceasefire line.
  • Captain Raja Muhammad Sarwar became the first recipient of the Nishan-e-Haider.

1965 war

  • Followed the Rann of Kutch clash (April) and Operation Gibraltar in Kashmir. India attacked Lahore on 6 September, now Defence Day.
  • Major Raja Aziz Bhatti defended the BRB Canal; the tank battle at Chawinda stopped the main Indian thrust on Sialkot.
  • The PAF (M. M. Alam) and the Navy (Operation Dwarka) also distinguished themselves. The Tashkent Declaration followed in 1966.

1971 war

  • Fought on both wings. It ended with the separation of East Pakistan, which became Bangladesh.
  • Four Nishan-e-Haider were earned in the war: Major Shabbir Sharif, Major Muhammad Akram, Sawar Muhammad Hussain and Lance Naik Muhammad Mahfuz. Pilot Officer Rashid Minhas had earned his in August 1971. PNS Hangor sank INS Khukri.
  • The Simla Agreement (1972) followed, and the ceasefire line became the Line of Control.

1999: Kargil

  • Fought on the heights of the Kargil sector. Captain Karnal Sher Khan and Havildar Lalak Jan earned the Nishan-e-Haider.

Counter-terrorism operations

  • Rah-e-Rast (Swat, 2009), Rah-e-Nijat (South Waziristan, 2009), Zarb-e-Azb (North Waziristan, 2014), Radd-ul-Fasaad (nationwide, 2017).

Recent

  • February 2019: Operation Swift Retort — the PAF shot down Indian aircraft after the Balakot strike.
  • May 2025: Operation Bunyan-um-Marsoos — Pakistan's response to Indian strikes. Read up on the latest details before your interview.
