For parents

Your son wants to become an army officer. Here is what the journey looks like and how you can help.

The journey

  • Registration → initial tests → physical and medical → initial interview → ISSB (4–5 days) → final merit and medical → about two years at PMA Kakul → commission as Second Lieutenant.

How you can help

  • Encourage daily running and exercise; fitness is tested from the first stage.
  • Get his eyes, feet, ears and teeth checked early.
  • Let him take responsibility at home — ISSB looks for it.
  • Talk with him about news and current affairs.
  • Don't pressure him; confidence matters more than fear of failure.

Important

Selection is on merit only. Anyone asking for money or ‘contacts’ to get a candidate selected is a fraud.

  • Many officers were recommended on their second or third attempt. One result is not the end.
