Interview syllabus — what gets asked

The Deputy President's interview usually lasts 20–40 minutes and is built on your PIQ (biodata form). Prepare these areas in this order.

The areas

  • Your PIQ and family — Everything you wrote: hometown, family, education, marks, games, hobbies. Expect follow-up questions on each line.
  • Education — Favourite and weak subjects, reasons for low marks, gaps or changes of field.
  • Games and hobbies — Rules, positions, famous players and recent results for your game; titles and authors for reading.
  • Pakistan affairs — Major national issues, the economy, water, energy, population — and your own sensible solutions.
  • Current and regional affairs — Relations with India, Afghanistan, Iran and China; Kashmir; recent major events. Read a newspaper daily.
  • Pakistan Studies and history — The Pakistan Movement, key dates and leaders, the constitutions, the wars.
  • Geography — Your own district and province, rivers, dams, passes, neighbours and borders, world capitals.
  • The armed forces — Ranks, arms and services, training academies, Nishan-e-Haider recipients, recent operations.
  • World organisations — The UN and its agencies, OIC, SAARC, SCO, ECO — what they do and where they are based.
  • Mental maths and common sense — Quick sums without paper: speed and time, percentages, unitary method, BODMAS.
  • Situations and character — What would you do if…? Honesty, loyalty to friends versus rules, handling failure.

How to prepare

  • Fill the PIQ in this app exactly as you will at the board, then practise the personalised interview it creates.
  • Read one newspaper a day and note three points on each major issue.
  • Practise mental maths out loud. The interviewer will not give you paper.
  • If you do not know an answer, say so. Guessing confidently and being wrong is worse.
