Physical test standards

Past official notices have used these minimum standards. Check the current notice — and aim well above the minimum, because better performance earns better marks.

Minimum standards (recent notices)

  • 1.6 km run in 8 minutes
  • 15 push-ups in 2 minutes
  • 20 sit-ups in 2 minutes
  • 3 chin-ups in 2 minutes
  • Ditch crossing: 7 ft 4 in × 7 ft 4 in, 4 ft deep

Safe targets

  • Run under 7:00, 40+ push-ups, 40+ sit-ups, 8+ chin-ups

Form matters

  • Push-ups: straight line from shoulders to heels; lower under control.
  • Sit-ups: practise the exact style announced on the day.
  • Chin-ups: start from a still hang; don't swing.

Follow the 8-week plan and log every test in the PT tracker.
