The psychologist’s interview

Short, personal, and built from your own psych answers.

Some candidates are called for a short interview with the psychologist. It is based on your own answers in the psychological tests — your stories, your sentences, your word associations — and on your biodata.

What it is about

  • Clearing up answers that were unclear or contradictory.
  • Understanding your feelings about family, friends, failure and pressure.
  • Checking that the person in the tests and the person in front of him are the same.

How to handle it

  • Answer briefly and honestly. If you wrote something negative, explain what it meant.
  • Do not panic: being called is not a bad sign. Many recommended candidates are called.
  • Do not try to guess “the right answer”. There isn’t one.
