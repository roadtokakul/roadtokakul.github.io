For women: every way to wear the uniform

Army, Navy and Air Force — what is open to you, and what ISSB is like for women.

Women serve as officers in all three services — as doctors, engineers, teachers, administrators, nurses and pilots. The way in depends on what you studied and when you apply.

Entries open to women

  • Army — Lady Cadet Course (graduates), Army Medical College (FSc Pre-Medical), Armed Forces Nursing Service, and direct entries for doctors and some specialists.
  • Navy — Short Service Commission in the branches named for women, medical entries.
  • Air Force — GD Pilot (women have graduated as PAF pilots since 2006), aeronautical engineering, Air Defence, Admin & Special Duties, Logistics, Accounts, Education and medical entries.

Open “Every way in” and tap “Women” to see only the entries open to you.

ISSB for women

  • The tests are the same in spirit: intelligence tests, psychological tests, group tasks, outdoor tasks and the interview.
  • Women are accommodated separately and examined by female medical staff.
  • Outdoor tasks are adjusted to the women’s course — you are judged on courage, effort and teamwork, not on lifting the heaviest log.
  • Wear simple, modest clothes: shalwar kameez with dupatta for formal sessions, a tracksuit and joggers for outdoor tasks. Nothing flashy.

What assessors look for

The same qualities as in men: honesty, initiative, the ability to cooperate and lead, staying calm under pressure, and a clear reason for joining. Do not try to sound tougher than you are — sound like yourself on your best day.

Rules on age, marital status and disciplines change with each advert. Always read the current advert on the official website.
