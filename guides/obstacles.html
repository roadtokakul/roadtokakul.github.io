Individual obstacles — technique for each

You get a fixed time (often about three minutes) to attempt as many obstacles as you can, each carrying points. Plan your order before you start, and do not waste time on one you keep failing.

Technique

  • Rope climb — grip high, lock the rope between your feet or knees, push with the legs and pull with the arms in turn.
  • Ditch / long jump — run in fast, look at the far side, never into the ditch.
  • Monkey crawl / rope bridge — keep your head up, move hand and foot alternately, keep momentum.
  • Tarzan swing — grip the rope as high as you can, swing, and let go at the far end of the swing.
  • Tyre — hold the rope above the tyre and slide through legs first.
  • Balance beam / zig-zag — look ahead along the beam, not down, and keep moving.
  • Wall (about 7 ft) — run in, plant a foot on the wall, grab the top with both hands in one movement, then swing a leg over.
  • High jump / plank jump — keep your balance on the take-off plank and commit to the jump.

Before the board

  • Practise pull-ups, rope work and short sprints for at least six weeks.
  • Know which obstacles you are weakest at and practise those most.
  • Fear of heights is common. Climb a little higher each practice session.
