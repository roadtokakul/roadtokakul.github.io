The three services at a glance

Pakistan Army

  • Fighting arms: Infantry and Armoured Corps
  • Supporting arms: Artillery, Air Defence, Engineers, Signals, Aviation
  • Services: Army Service Corps, Ordnance, EME, Army Medical Corps and others
  • Officer training: PMA Kakul, Abbottabad. Motto: Iman, Taqwa, Jihad fi Sabilillah

Pakistan Navy

  • Branches include Operations, Marine Engineering, Weapon Engineering, Supply and Medical
  • Officer training: Pakistan Naval Academy (PNS Rahbar), Karachi. Navy Day: 8 September

Pakistan Air Force

  • Branches include General Duties (Pilot), Air Defence, Engineering, Logistics, Administration and others
  • Officer training: PAF Academy Asghar Khan, Risalpur. PAF Day: 7 September

Equivalent ranks (Army — Navy — Air Force)

  • Second Lieutenant — Midshipman — Pilot Officer
  • Lieutenant — Sub Lieutenant — Flying Officer
  • Captain — Lieutenant — Flight Lieutenant
  • Major — Lieutenant Commander — Squadron Leader
  • Lieutenant Colonel — Commander — Wing Commander
  • Colonel — Captain — Group Captain
  • Brigadier — Commodore — Air Commodore
  • Major General — Rear Admiral — Air Vice Marshal
  • Lieutenant General — Vice Admiral — Air Marshal
  • General — Admiral — Air Chief Marshal

Gallantry awards, highest first

  • Nishan-e-Haider, Hilal-e-Jurat, Sitara-e-Jurat, Tamgha-e-Jurat
