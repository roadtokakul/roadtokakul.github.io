Road to Kakul — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Road to Kakul is an independent preparation app for candidates applying to the Pakistan Army, Navy and Air Force. It is not affiliated with any armed force, ISSB or academy.

What stays on your phone

Your name, chosen course, practice results, PT logs, written answers, the oath you sign and your progress are stored only on your device. We do not run a server that collects them, and you need no account. The exceptions are listed below: payments, questions and chat messages you choose to send, the notification address if you turn message notifications on, and anonymous usage counts.

Payments

You can buy a pass with Easypaisa and enter the Transaction ID from your receipt. So that payments can be checked, the app sends that Transaction ID, the pass you chose, the name you typed in the app, your course, a random device ID and the time to the Road to Kakul team's private Google Form (stored by Google). If you apply for the honour scholarship, your written reasons are sent the same way. Nothing else leaves your phone, and we never see your Easypaisa PIN, account or bank details.

In the Android app, Premium can also be bought through Google Play. Google processes that payment and tells the app only whether you own Premium.

Unlocking the Officer’s Library works the same way: the Transaction ID, the amount, your course, a random device ID and the time go to the same private Google Form so the payment can be checked.

Questions you send

If you use “Ask the team”, the app sends your question, its topic, your course, a random device ID and the time to the same private Google Form. The team reads the questions and publishes answers to the most asked ones inside the app, without saying who asked.

Community chat

If you join the Community chat, the messages you write are shown, with the nickname you choose, to everyone who opens the chat. They are sent through the same Google Form, with a random device ID, so the chat can work and abuse can be stopped; other users see only a short code made from that ID, never the ID itself. Phone numbers, links and email addresses are hidden automatically. Do not share personal details in the chat. The team can remove messages and block a phone from the chat.

Anonymous usage counts

Once a day the app sends a short anonymous note to the same Google Form when it is opened (with how many practice questions were answered on that phone the day before), the first time it is opened on a phone, and when the ISSB section, the free taster or the passes page is opened, with your course, a random device ID and how the app was opened (installed app or browser). It contains no name, messages, answers or phone number. The team uses these counts only to see which parts help aspirants and where they stop, and to show real totals such as how many aspirants practised this week.

Ads

Free users can choose to watch rewarded ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar data to show and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules a notification on your phone. No data is sent anywhere.

Message notifications (a team message, a new answer, news, new chat messages) are off until you turn them on. When they are on, your phone's browser gives the app a notification address, made by Google or Apple for this app on this phone. It is sent through the same Google Form with a random device ID and no name, so the team can tell your phone that something new arrived. Each notification is put together on your phone from the public chat, the team's answers and the news. Turning notifications off removes the address.

Content updates

When online, the app downloads a public file of news and practice questions from GitHub. This request contains no personal information.

Children

The app is meant for candidates aged 16 and over and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.
